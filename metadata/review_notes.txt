The only usage-description string in the build settings is for the camera and reads: This app does not use the camera. That prompt never appears, because the app does not request camera access and does not use the camera. On a cold launch the system may show a notification permission prompt. There is no microphone, photos, or location prompt.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Phreatic is a personal hydration log. It is for people who want a falling water table as a reminder to drink. The well starts full at wake. A sip stems the ebb. Staying above the stave is the day.

No credentials or demo account are needed. Cold launch opens The well starts full, with the line At wake the water table is at the daily goal. It falls through the hours you are up. Tap Continue. The next page is Stem the ebb, with the line Log a sip to push the table back up. Stay above the stave and the day is held. Tap Continue. The next page is Body weight, with the line The daily well is sized from your weight. This is a personal target, not medical advice. Set the wheel and tap Continue. The last page is Waking hours, with Wake and Sleep. Tap Continue. Skip on any page writes defaults and goes to the well. Home is titled Phreatic and shows remaining millilitres and how much is left before you fall behind today. If the day has no sips, The day just started and First glass, best start appear. Tap Log a sip. After that, the well stays on screen with a stave band labeled Still ahead or Behind and Open history, plus Still ahead of today's line or Behind today's line. Drain now, Today's sips, and Last 7 days sit with the well. Tap Open history to open History. The header Days behind you shows the count. Each seal is Held or Fell behind. Tap a day to see Sip rows with volumes and times, Fell behind, and Caught up, then tap Close. From the well, tap Activity doubles the drain for one hour to open Ebb drain. That sheet is titled The well drains while you wait and shows Safe or Ebb. Tap Close. On the well, tap Double the drain to log activity for one hour. Tap the gear labeled Settings to open Settings. Body and hours has Body weight, Wake, and Sleep. Activity bonus offers 0, 350, and 700, with the line Still water, a stirred day, or a doubled draw. Default sip sets Tap volume. Daily goal is a read-only millilitre line. Sources explains the 33 millilitres per kilogram figure. Contact Phreatic is the support control. Walk through setup again reopens the first-launch pages. Clear today and Reset all data ask for confirmation. Tap Close to return to the well.

Sips, activity, and settings stay on this device. Contact Phreatic in Settings opens the support page.

Behavior does not change by region. Times, dates, and numbers follow the device locale.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. This is a personal hydration log, not a medical device.

Settings has a Sources section. The 33 millilitres per kilogram figure is explained there, and the copy states this app is not medical advice. Water in diet and Dietary Reference Intakes for Water are tappable links. Body weight setup also states this is a personal target, not medical advice.
