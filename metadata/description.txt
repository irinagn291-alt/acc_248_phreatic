Phreatic is a personal hydration log. The well starts full at wake. The water table falls through your waking hours, and a sip stems the ebb.

• Set Body weight and Waking hours (Wake and Sleep), or tap Skip. The Daily goal is sized from your weight plus an Activity bonus of 0, 350, or 700 millilitres.
• Watch remaining millilitres on the well. The water table falls in real time until Sleep.
• Tap Log a sip to add the Default sip volume and push the table back up.
• Tap Double the drain to double the fall for one hour.
• The stave sits at 30 percent of the Daily goal. Still ahead and Behind mark Safe and Ebb.
• Tap Open history to see Days behind you, days that Held or Fell behind, sip times, Fell behind, and Caught up.
• Open Settings from the gear to change Body and hours, Activity bonus, and Default sip, read Sources, or tap Contact Phreatic.
• A new day shows The day just started and First glass, best start.

Phreatic is a personal log. It is not medical advice.
